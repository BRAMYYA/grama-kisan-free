What is the difference between MLA, MP and MLC? (తేడా ఏమిటి?)

MLA: represents an Assembly constituency in the state legislature. MP: serves in Parliament, in Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha. MLC: serves in the state Legislative Council; seats may represent different electorates or be nominated. A village or PIN code alone does not identify one universal MLC.
